Core Principles

Never skip breakfast. Eat every 3–4 hours for stable blood sugar. Carbs concentrated around training.

Programme Structure

Each gym day has 2 sections. Friends section (shared compounds) + solo stay-back (10–15 min, priority work). The stay-back is where physique priorities live, short enough to always do it.

Maingain rule. Weight creeping up fast or waist wider, pull back 100–150 kcal. Weight dropping, add 100 kcal from carbs.

Recovery Rules

Cap strain ≤12 on any day recovery <60%.
Don’t stack hard training day after low-recovery night, Tuesday crashes trace to weekend push.
Sprints (1×/wk) planned post-bloodwork window.
Sleep consistency beats sleep duration for your recovery numbers.
